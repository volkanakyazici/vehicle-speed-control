## Python numerical checks

Environment: Python 3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5, Matplotlib 3.10.8.

The numerical reference covers all 12 baseline combinations. The checks in `python/checks.py` passed during repository review:

- Flat-road resistance at 20 m/s is 332.89 N.
- The numerical P equilibrium agrees with the analytic positive root of the force-balance equation.
- Applied force remains within the specified limits.
- Speeds remain finite and positive.
- Halving the integration step from 0.01 to 0.005 s changes sampled speed by less than 0.05 m/s in all 12 baseline combinations.
- Flat-road PI removes the steady-state speed offset.
- PI_AW recovers into the defined error band earlier than plain PI in the windup scenario.

The step-size comparison is a forward-Euler refinement check, not a comparison against an independent high-order solver.

## Scope

These checks establish consistency of the implementations, archived data and simplified equations for the tested configurations.

They do not establish agreement with a real vehicle, hardware performance, optimal tuning or robustness across untested parameters. The model has no active braking, actuator dynamics, tire-slip model or measured vehicle calibration.
